Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Q: How do I purchase time in the app?

A: You can purchase time credits through the in-app purchase system. Select 'Buy Time' in the app and choose the amount of time you want to add.

Q: How can I check my remaining time?

A: Your remaining time is displayed on the main screen of the app.

Q: What happens if I run out of time during use?

A: You'll need to purchase additional time to continue using the app's features.

Q: Can I get a refund for unused time?

A: For refund requests, please contact Apple Support through the App Store.
